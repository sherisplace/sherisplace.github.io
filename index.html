Since 2010

Sheri opened the doors on Main Street in 2010 with a simple idea: good food, good friends, and a place that feels like home. More than a decade later, that hasn't changed.

Come in from the cold, grab a seat by the fireplace, and bring the whole crew — kids included. Whether it's burgers and wings or a sizzling Tex-Mex plate, we'll treat you like a regular from your very first visit.
