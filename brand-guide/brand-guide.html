Sheri's Place · A family restaurant on Main Street · Since 2010
30 Main St, Springfield, VT · Colors, type, logos and elements for the website, menus and everything after
01 · THE FEEL

Like walking into a neighbor's kitchen.

Sheri's is a warm, unfussy, family place on Main Street: brick walls, a fireplace, comfort food and Tex-Mex, and people who know your name. Everything we make should feel cozy first and polished second, and it should always be easy to use.

Warm, not slick

Brick reds, butter golds and cream paper. Rounded corners, soft shadows. Nothing cold, gray or corporate.

Easy above all

Big tap targets, plain words, the phone number and directions always one tap away. If someone has to hunt, we've failed.

A little bit of sparkle

The gold script logo is the showpiece. Let it shine on dark brick; keep everything around it calm.

Real, not stock

Use real photos of the building, the food and Sheri. Never stock photography or AI-looking imagery.

How we sound

Say it like this

  • "Pull up a chair — you're family here."
  • "Hand cut fries, homemade marinara."
  • "Have an allergy? Just let your server know."
  • Short sentences. Friendly. Specific about the food.

Steer clear of

  • Emojis, anywhere.
  • Small red ALL-CAPS labels stacked above headings ("WHAT FOLKS ARE SAYING"). They read as generic and templated.
  • Prices on the website (keep them on the printed menu).
  • Buzzwords like "elevated," "curated" or "experience."
02 · LOGOS

The family of marks

Three marks, each with a job. The gold script is the headliner, the S is the shorthand, and the illustrated badge is the hometown classic.

S MONOGRAM

Gold script

The main logo. Website header, menu covers, signage, merch. Best on Brick or dark backgrounds where the gold glows.

S monogram

Small spaces: the browser icon, social avatars, stamps, napkins, the top of a menu page, cup sleeves.

Heritage badge

The illustrated portrait of Sheri on the building sign. Use for "our story" moments and nostalgia pieces, not as the main logo.

03 · USING THE LOGO

Give it room to breathe

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the heart. Nothing (text, edges, photos) should cross into that zone.

Ideas to make it sick

  • Front cover: gold script logo on the brick gradient with the storefront faded in behind it, just like the website.
  • Give "Sheri's famous" items and house favorites a small gold S monogram, and repeat that S on the website.
  • Back page: hours, events and a QR code to the website and email list.
  • Put a real photo of Sheri on the beverages page with a one-line "since 2010" story.
  • Get the logo redrawn as vector art (SVG/PDF). The current files are images and will go soft past about 6 in wide.
